```json

```

Its real shape belongs to the Evaluation Structure and must be supplied by the developer requirements/runtime input.

An evaluation may additionally contain a numeric `weight` when expert weights are used.

Criteria-weighting models use the same top-level request contract but do not use issue-model alternatives in their creator-side context.
